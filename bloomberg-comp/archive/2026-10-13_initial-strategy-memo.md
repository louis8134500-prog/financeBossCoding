## Sizing and risk rules

Cap each name at 20% and cut any position that falls 8% below entry. Everything else follows from those two rules.

1. **Position cap:** 20% of portfolio value per name (competition rule). Trim back to 20% when a winner drifts above it.
2. **Stop:** sell if a name closes 8% below entry, or closes below its 20-day moving average after a failed earnings reaction. No averaging down.
3. **Earnings exposure:** never hold more than 35% of the book through prints on the same night.
4. **Theme cap:** memory (SK hynix + Micron + Sandisk + Samsung + Kioxia) at most 50% combined.
5. **Cash floor and event sizing:** 10% at all times; 20% the day before CPI and FOMC. A position opened in the 24 hours before either release goes on at half size; add the rest after it.
6. **Drawdown brake:** if the portfolio is down 7% from its peak, cut gross exposure to 60% for two sessions and re-enter only on catalysts.
7. **Endgame (Nov 6–13), set by standings:**
   - Ahead of the field: cut to 60–70% gross and protect the lead.
   - Behind the field: stay fully invested in the 2–3 strongest trends; finishing mid-pack is the same as losing.
8. **Execution:** use limit orders; for Korea, Taiwan and Japan lines, place orders in local hours and check FX on the terminal. Korea and Japan trade 8 PM–2:30 AM ET and Taiwan 9 PM–1:30 AM ET, the evening before the local date. Those times move one hour earlier once US clocks fall back on Nov 1. A US-morning release such as CPI first reaches Asian prices that evening.

## Strengths, weaknesses and timing

The edge is catalyst timing on the strongest earnings theme in the index. The main risk is that everyone else owns the same theme.

|  | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Strengths | Trades the sector with the biggest estimate raises; every position has a dated catalyst and a planned exit; staggered entries recycle capital through the earnings cluster |
| Weaknesses | Memory and AI hardware are crowded: a "beat but sell" reaction is likely on some prints; the book is one theme, so a broad AI selloff hits every position at once |
| Opportunities | TSMC lagged peers, so its bar is low; Sandisk and Kioxia move 10%+ on NAND pricing news; a "Fed is done" signal on Oct 28 would lift high-multiple names |
| Threats | Hot CPI or another hike; a hyperscaler cutting capex guidance (Oct 27–29); an Iran ceasefire knocking oil and rotating money out of momentum |

**Timing by holding period**

- **Day trades:** not the edge. Use them only to exit on earnings reactions, never to open positions.
- **Multi-day (2–5 days):** the core. TSMC, Lam and Celestica are each bought 1–5 days before a catalyst and sold into it.
- **Weekly or longer:** SK hynix and Micron are trend holds through the window, sized at the top of the book and protected by the stop rules.
