## If/then rules

1. **Stop hit:** sell the whole position next session (US names by 10:30 AM); reinvest in the strongest name with room within a day.
2. **First target:** sell a third and move the stop to cost. **Second target:** sell another third and move the stop to the first target. Reinvest within a day.
3. **Up 3% or more on the day an order is due:** buy half, and the rest at 3:30 PM.
4. **Ceasefire, or Brent down 8% in a day** (`CO1 Comdty`): sell half of every energy and defense position that day; move the cash into AI names with room, or a name the team agrees on. Above $115 Brent, hold energy but add nothing.
5. **Nasdaq-100 down more than 1.5% at 10:30 AM on an AI-order day:** wait a day; energy and defense orders go ahead.
6. **Memory stays at zero** until it stops falling after SK hynix reports, about Oct 27.
